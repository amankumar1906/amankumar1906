## What I’m building 🔧

**[Walkie One](https://walkieone.com)** is a cellular handheld for talking to your
AI assistant. Hold the talk button, say what you need, and press play when the
reply is ready.

Lately, that’s meant working on firmware, voice recording and playback,
Claude Code and Codex connections, the macOS app, and the website.
Lots of little pieces between “button pressed” and “reply played.”

[Browse the code](https://github.com/santasailab/walkie-one) · [Hardware & case files](https://walkieone.com/open-source) · [Run your own server](https://github.com/santasailab/walkie-one/blob/main/personal-server/README.md)
